Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

My Room Link helps people find a room, flat or house to rent, and helps landlords list properties. This page explains what information we collect when you use the site, why, and what rights you have over it.

Who we are

My Room Link is the service you're using. If you have any question about your information or want to exercise any of the rights below, the quickest way to reach us is on WhatsApp or by phone — both are on the site's homepage and in the footer of every page.

What we collect, and why

We only collect what we need to do the thing you've asked us to do.

If you're looking for a room (the "Find me a room" form):
  • Your name and phone number, so we can match you to a room and get in touch.
  • Your email, if you give one — an extra way to reach you.
  • The area and size of home you're after, so we only match you to relevant places.
  • Whether you're claiming benefits, since most of the properties we list require Housing Benefit or Universal Credit to cover the cost — this helps us check you're eligible before we put your time into matching you.
  • Anything extra you choose to tell us in the notes field (e.g. needing an ensuite or step-free access).
If you're listing a property ("List your property" form):
  • Your phone number, so we can follow up about the listing.
  • Basic details about the property — rooms, postcode, features — to put together a draft listing.

We use this information only to run the matching service itself: finding you a room, or finding a tenant for your property. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Our legal basis for using it

We process your details because you've asked us to help you find a room or list a property — this is "performance of a contract" at your request. Where that doesn't quite apply (for example, keeping a record after you've been matched, so we can follow up), we rely on our legitimate interest in running the service properly, which doesn't override your own rights, set out below.

Who we share it with

  • Landlords — if a property looks like a good match for you, we share your name and phone number with that landlord so they can arrange a viewing. We don't share your information with anyone else unless we're matching you to a specific place.
  • Our service providers — the systems that store this information and send notification emails on our behalf (our database and email providers) can access it only to provide those technical services, not for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you might still be looking for a room, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you get in touch again. If you'd like us to delete your information sooner, just ask — see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us at any time to:

  • See what information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop using your information, or object to how we're using it.

To do any of this, message us on WhatsApp or call the number on the homepage — we'll action it promptly. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.

Security

Your information is stored on secure, encrypted systems, and our own staff access to it is protected by a login only we hold.

Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Nothing you do on this site is shared with advertisers.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we do with your information changes, and update the date at the top.
